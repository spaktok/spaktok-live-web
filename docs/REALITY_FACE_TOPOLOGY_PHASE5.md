# SpakTok Reality Engine — Phase 5: Dense Face Topology & Surface Attachment

2026-10-27 — Implemented as a **device-local, QA-gated** incremental subsystem.

## Actual tracking geometry
- MediaPipe FaceLandmarker returns up to 478 tracked facial/iris landmarks
  on supported model builds. This is **not** a 478-point digital skull scan.
- The physically rendered facial surface is generated from
  `FaceLandmarker.FACE_LANDMARKS_TESSELATION` connection triples.
  MediaPipe's directed triangle winding, dense vertex adjacency and
  triangle-by-vertex memberships are preserved; do not reconstruct polygons
  from arbitrary sorted cliques.
- A single, shared oriented triangle index buffer now serves the depth
  occluder and the tracked cosmetic face surface.
- The semantic face atlas registers MediaPipe official lips, both eyes,
  eyebrows, iris points and face oval, plus approximate forehead, jaw,
  cheek, chin and nose clusters expanded via REAL mesh adjacency.

## Stable 3D surface binding
- `reality-face-surface.ts` assigns a one-time triangle ID plus three
  barycentric weights from a named semantic region and normalized target.
  Those weights are conserved when all three detected triangle vertices
  move under head rotation, translation or facial expression.
- Every frame, the attachment reprojects the three tracked XYZ landmarks
  through exactly the same object-fit cover, single selfie X mirror and
  depth scaling as the depth occluder. A front-facing 3D surface normal
  supplies a controlled camera-facing offset.
- Degenerate triangles, missing landmarks and invalid inputs return
  `null` and hide an unsafe attachment; stale positions are never reused.
- The crystal GLB is the *first* declarative `surfaceBinding` example.
  The deployed default still uses its stable original forehead head anchor;
  enable `?realitySurface=1` for dense surface attachment QA.

## Dense tracking visualization
- `?realityMesh=1` enables a translucent wireframe of the REAL triangulated
  depth surface, with the same live 478 landmarks, rotation and deformation
  as the cosmetic layer. This is a development diagnostic, not a filter.
- No fake points are generated by this diagnostic. It shows only detected
  mesh triangles, not inferred full skull/scalp geometry.
- `?realityGpu=2&realitySurface=1&realityMesh=1` combines GPU v2, the
  experimental surface attachment and the tracking overlay for QA.

## Engineering guarantees and current limits
- FaceLandmarker may return facial transformation matrices and expression
  blendshape scores. These are estimates, not calibrated metric ground truth.
- This phase does **not** add new face-detection models, universal multi-face,
  real measured 3D head scan, proper ear/hair segmentation, hands occlusion
  or full CapCut-like timeline/editor authoring UI.
- Realistic effects additionally require professional GLB geometry,
  UV/PBR textures, scene lighting, proper physical materials, calibration,
  rigging, render quality and end-to-end tests on Android/iOS hardware.
- The module and tests are architectural building blocks for a professional
  authoring platform, not a claim of feature parity with TikTok or Snapchat.
